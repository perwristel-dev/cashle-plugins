# Finnish public pension

Finnish statutory earnings-related old-age pension (*työeläke*) with an optional estimate of the national and guarantee pensions paid by Kela (*kansaneläke* and *takuueläke*). Cashle shows the same record in **Savings** and **Income**. The Savings row shows the gross earnings-related entitlement in EUR per month. The Income row controls the lifetime payout. The entitlement creates no investment balance, inheritance, or withdrawal source.

## Inputs

- Select the individual pension recipient. Cashle uses their birth year and month.
- Enter the gross monthly earnings-related pension from the pension record or a provider's forecast in euros at the start of the plan. Use an amount that already reflects the life expectancy coefficient and excludes any future deferral bonus. When the pension is already in payment, enter the current gross amount and mark it as already receiving in Income.
- Pick the earliest full pension, an age, a date, or an existing milestone. A request before the applicable minimum moves to the earliest eligible month. An age choice starts in the month after the selected birthday. A date after the first of a month moves to the next month. For employees, select a time by which the employment they retire from will have ended.
- Enable the Kela estimate for living alone or with a partner when the recipient qualifies for the full residence-based amount. The optional other monthly pension amount covers other gross pensions that count for both Kela benefits. Enter that income separately in the case if it should also appear in cash flow.

## Calculation

The general retirement age rises by three months for each cohort from 1955 through 1962. It is 65 for the 1962–1965 cohorts. Full employment pension may first start at the beginning of the month after reaching the minimum age. For 1966 and later, the module estimates the age in months by interpolating ETK's published 2026 five-year cohort projections. It uses the last published table age of 69 for cohorts after 2010 and applies the general rules to older cohorts. A deferred earnings pension increases by 0.4% per month. [ETK old-age pension](https://www.etk.fi/en/finnish-pension-system/pensions/earnings-related-pension-benefits/old-age-pension/) · [1965 confirmation](https://www.tyoelake.fi/en/news/retirement-age-confirmed-for-those-born-in-1965/) · [ETK long-term projections](https://www.etk.fi/en/research-statistics-and-projections/projections/long-term-projections/latest-projections/) · [Pension record and life expectancy coefficient](https://www.tyoelake.fi/en/how-much-pension/life-expectancy-coefficient-applied-when-pension-starts/)

The Kela estimate starts at the later of the chosen pension start and the regular Kela age. That age is 65 for recipients born before 1965 and follows their earnings-related cohort age from 1965. The national pension uses the household rate, pension-income disregard, half taper and minimum payable amount. It excludes the earnings-related deferral bonus from the national pension reduction and applies Kela's own deferral bonus of 0.6% per month before the 1962 cohort and 0.4% for later cohorts. The guarantee pension subtracts the actual gross earnings-related pension, national pension and entered other pensions from the guarantee maximum, subject to its minimum payment. The model uses the published 2026 and 2027 rates and indexes the minimum payment with the national pension index. It suspends the Kela estimate during modeled non-Finnish residence. [Kela old-age pension](https://www.kela.fi/old-age-pension) · [Kela guarantee pension](https://www.kela.fi/guarantee-pension) · [Kela benefit rates for 2026 and 2027](https://www.kela.fi/operations-national-pensions-index)

The estimate posts on the first day of each eligible month and stops at the recipient's life milestone. It uses plan inflation for future January adjustments to the statement amount and any unpublished Kela rates. The simulation uses its case exchange rates to convert EUR. The gross receipt uses the `pension` tax basis. The optional Finnish tax plugin handles pension-only income and currently rejects cases that declare another earned-income class such as salary or benefits alongside pension. [ETK pension taxation](https://www.etk.fi/en/finnish-pension-system/pensions/taxation-of-pensions/)

Use a provider forecast to capture future wage accrual, pensionable employment and the life expectancy adjustment. The module covers the standard full pension and regular-age Kela estimate. Partial old-age pension, Kela early or historically adjusted awards, residence proration, occupational exceptions, survivor and disability pensions, exact benefit payment days, and international tax treaties are outside this scope. Kela's statutory index and the separate employment pension indexes can move differently from plan inflation. [Partial old-age pension](https://www.tyoelake.fi/en/different-pensions/flexible-partial-old-age-pension/) · [Statutory pension indices](https://www.etk.fi/en/finnish-pension-system/pensions/pension-indexation/earnings-related-pension-indexes/)

## Direct model

Run `cashle plugins/finnish-public-pension/doc/direct-model.cashle --classpath plugins/finnish-public-pension` from the catalog repository root. The person requests age 64 but first qualifies in February 2027, the month after turning 65. With a €200 earnings-related pension, full Kela eligibility and zero inflation, the gross amount is €1,013.39 per month from February through April.
