# Canadian federal public service pension

Use the federal Pension Centre estimate for the member's chosen departure date
and pension option. In Savings, enter the pensionable service at departure and
the separate gross annual lifetime and bridge amounts, with any reduction
already included in the quotes. In Income, choose the payment start and option
on the same pension record. Pension amounts are in plan-start Canadian dollars.

| Membership group | Earliest unreduced age with 2 to fewer than 30 years | Earliest unreduced age with at least 30 years | Earliest reduced annual allowance age |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1, on or before December 31, 2012 | 60 | 55 | 50 |
| Group 2, on or after January 1, 2013 | 65 | 60 | 55 |

The first full projected month is no earlier than the month after the
applicable birthday, with the age-71 calendar year December limit for a
registered defined benefit. Date and milestone starts round to the next full
month when midmonth. The projection pays the lifetime amount monthly through
the recipient's life and includes the bridge through the age-65 birthday
month even if CPP or QPP starts at a different time. It uses the plan's
inflation to approximate January indexation and converts from CAD via plan FX.

Use an official quote for any change to the departure or claim date. Service
accrual, contributions, annual allowance reductions, disability or survivor
benefits, transfer values, partial first months, re-employment, and special
unreduced early departures are outside this projection. For an existing
pension, choose a current or historical payment start and enter current gross
amounts. Use zero for the bridge if disability entitlement ended it.

The payments go to the registered pension tax basis. Enable the Canadian tax
plugin with registered pension support for ordinary Alberta or Ontario tax,
including the pension income amount. The [direct model](doc/direct-model.cashle)
shows the bridge ending even while the lifetime benefit continues.
