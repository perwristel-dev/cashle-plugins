# Australian residential transfer duty

Estimate and schedule transfer duty for an ordinary purchase of an
established owner-occupied home in New South Wales or Victoria. Choose the
state where the land is located and enter the contract date. Link a planned
home with a fixed purchase date to take its settlement date and purchase
price, or enter settlement and dutiable value directly.

Duty is based on the greater of consideration and unencumbered market value.
For a linked home, set an explicit nominal AUD dutiable value if it exceeds
the modeled purchase price. The modeled price follows the property's
nominal or inflation-adjusted purchase setting and the plan's FX snapshot.
The expense follows the linked settings and is funded separately from the
purchase. It can still post if the purchase fails for lack of funds in a
projection. Update or disable the expense if the purchase is cancelled.

- New South Wales uses the schedule effective on the contract date. The
  package includes 2025–26 and 2026–27 and uses the current table for later
  projections. NSW fiscal-year brackets are indexed annually. For residential
  land at most two hectares the premium tier begins above AUD 3.87 million
  in 2026–27. Payment is scheduled on the earlier of settlement and three
  months after signing.
- Victoria uses the current general rates, including the tier above AUD
  2 million, and schedules payment at electronic settlement. The optional
  ordinary principal place of residence concession applies up to AUD 550,000.
  Confirm the SRO's eligibility rules, including moving in within 12 months
  of settlement and using the property as the principal home for at least
  12 consecutive months.

First-home schemes, off-the-plan and special purchaser concessions,
foreign purchaser surcharges, mixed-use land, unusually large NSW parcels,
registration fees, penalties and individual assessment rounding are outside
the estimate. Enter a separate expense for any other charge applicable to
the purchase.
