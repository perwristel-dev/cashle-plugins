# Finnish capital redemption contract

Use this for a personal EUR-denominated kapitalisaatiosopimus from a Finnish
insurer, with verified ordinary Income Tax Act §35 treatment. Enter the
contract's surrender value and remaining contributed principal from the
insurer's statement. Record current-year payments and prior withholding
using the insurer's actual figures. Keep investment returns inside the
contract until withdrawal.

The taxable share of a payment is the payment times the ratio of accrued
positive return to the total surrender value at that point. The remaining
payment returns principal. A partial withdrawal from a loss-making contract
returns principal without recognizing a loss. The remaining loss becomes
deductible on actual full surrender or maturity, when the contract closes.
An empty balance alone leaves the contract open.

The Finnish insurer withholds 30% of the taxable share when that share
exceeds EUR 20. The owner’s ordinary annual Finnish tax assessment includes
the full taxable share, including payments below the withholding threshold.
The existing Finnish capital-income loss pool applies a closing loss against
capital income and carries unused loss into the next ten tax years. This loss
is excluded from the deficit credit against tax on earned income.

Use the standard profile where the insurer retains control of underlying
assets and the holder selects from the insurer's ordinary allocation
options. Owner-like voting, control over transfers or use, or trading orders
with third parties can bring the contract under §35b and remove the deferral.
Cashle rejects this profile. Separate surrender fees, jointly owned and
acquired contracts, third-party disposal, foreign contracts or tax residence,
and prior §35b tax adjustments are outside the supported projection.
